Our Story

From 11 people in a living room, to a church family today.

People's Church began in January 1981 in the living room of a home, with just 11 people at the first gathering. Since then, we've grown into a diverse church family serving the Peace Country — but our heart has never changed: everyone matters to God, and everyone matters to us.

Today, we're a multi-generational, multicultural church family — including a dedicated Korean-speaking congregation — united around one mission and one hope.

What We're About

Fellowship. Maturity. Ministry. Mission. Worship.

Five words from our mission statement that shape everything we do as a church.

Fellowship

Real relationships and belonging — doing life together, not just showing up on Sunday.

Maturity

Growing in faith through scripture, prayer, and community — for a lifetime, not just a season.

Ministry

Discovering the gifts God gave you, and using them to serve others well.

Mission

Reaching Grande Prairie and the Peace Country with the good news of Jesus, in word and deed.

Worship

Gathering to give God honor and thanks — with our voices, our lives, and our whole hearts.
