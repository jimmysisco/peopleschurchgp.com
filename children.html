People's Nursery · Age 0–2

People's Nursery

Sunday Morning
People's Church, Room 101–102

People's Nursery is a safe and nurturing environment for infants from Newborn – Age 2 to be cared for during our Sunday morning service! Our nursery is staffed with loving volunteers who are excited to shower your infants with God's love so that parents can enjoy our Sunday morning service knowing that their kids are in the best hands! The nursery has a crib room that is also outfitted with comfortable chairs for baby feeding, as well as two play rooms for crawlers or walkers!

We are always looking for volunteers for each area of childcare, so if you are interested please contact our Children's Pastor, Angela at angela.sisco@peopleschurchgp.com!

Little People's Church · Age 3–5

Little People's Church

Sunday Morning, 10:30–12:00
People's Church, Room 103–104

Every Sunday morning, from 10:30–12:00 we offer a super fun and engaging opportunity for your preschooler to learn about God in a fun, safe and loving environment! Kinder age kids have the option to stay in LPC or move up to Rooted Kids at The Reach for their kindergarten year. This classroom offers a fantastic hands on learning environment for your preschoolers each week!

A morning in LPC consists of a centre time, clean up, music time, Bible lesson/story time, snack time and a craft time as well! This classroom experience is an incredible opportunity for your preschooler to learn more about God and His word while engaging with loving volunteers and new friends each week! Please contact our Children's Pastor, Angela if you are interested in serving in this area at angela.sisco@peopleschurchgp.com!

Rooted Kids · Kindergarten–Grade 5

Welcome to Rooted Kids

Sunday Morning, 10:30–12:00
The Reach Centre

Rooted Kids is our Elementary age ministry! Each Sunday children from Kindergarten – Grade 5 are invited to join us at The Reach Centre from 10:30–12:00 for an engaging and exciting church service! We have lots of time to run and play on our fabulous Reach Play Centre and inflatables, followed by a time of worship, an incredible Bible lesson and a delicious snack! We would love to have your kiddo join us!

If you have any questions or are interested in serving in Children's, please contact our Children's Pastor, Angela at angela.sisco@peopleschurchgp.com.

Fun Friday Nights · Grade 1–5

Fun Friday Nights

Children from Grade 1–5 are invited out every second Friday to The Reach Centre for a fuel-burning night of fun, friendship and faith building! Fun Fridays are a drop off program for all kids from Grade 1–5 to come and play on our incredible play equipment, enjoy a delicious snack, engage in a life changing Bible story and enjoy a themed activity! The cost is $2 and it runs every second Friday throughout the school year!
