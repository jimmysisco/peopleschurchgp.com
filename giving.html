Ways to Give

Give in the Way That Works for You

However you choose to give, every gift makes a difference in our church family and community.

Online Giving

Give a one-time or recurring gift securely online through CanadaHelps. Create an account to save your card as a payment method for future giving.

Give Now

Pre-Authorized Auto Debit

Automatic debit from your bank account is available for your convenience. Fill out a Pre-Authorized Auto Debit form at Grand Central or the church office. Choose to give monthly (on the 1st or 15th), or weekly or biweekly.

Cash or Cheque

Envelopes for cash gifts are available at Grand Central — feel free to drop one in the basket during the offering. Cheques should be made out to People's Church and may be placed in the basket without an envelope, or mailed to the church office at 11850 – 108 Street, Grande Prairie, AB T8V 8G5.

Give Via PayPal

Prefer not to set up an account? You can make a gift through PayPal, which accepts Mastercard, Visa, American Express, Discover, and most bank cards. Contact the church office and our team will help set up your gift.

Debit or Credit Card

Debit machines are available at Grand Central on Sunday mornings and at the reception desk during the week — just fill out a tithing slip. You can also give by credit card by calling the church office; your card number is used for a one-time donation only and is never stored.

Give Stock

Making a gift of appreciated stock or mutual funds is a win-win — you receive a charitable tax deduction for the full fair market value, and it creates lasting impact for the church. If you're considering a gift of stock or securities, let us know so we can verify the transfer and properly record your donation.

Questions

Giving FAQ

Is my gift tax-deductible? +

Yes. People's Church (registered as Grande Prairie People's Full Gospel Assembly, Business No. 889304465RR0001) is a CRA-registered charity. Gifts made online through CanadaHelps come with an instant tax receipt.

When will I get my tax receipt? +

Official donation receipts are issued annually, in the third week of January for the previous year. Please make sure we have your current mailing address and an email address on file.

How do I get a tax receipt for an e-transfer or in-person gift? +

Include your full name and mailing address with your e-transfer or offering envelope, and our office will issue a receipt. Reach out to mail@peopleschurchgp.com if you have questions about a receipt.

Can my donation receipt be issued in both my name and my spouse's? +

Absolutely. Contact our office to be designated as joint givers. You'll then receive one statement with both your names and your combined giving total.

What's the best way to give to People's Church? +

Whatever is most convenient for you. Giving through cheque, cash, or online giving carries the lowest processing fee.

What ministry would benefit the most from my gift? +

The best way to support People's Church is by giving to the General Offering. This allows us to direct your gift where it's needed most.

Is giving online secure? +

Yes. Online gifts are processed through CanadaHelps, a registered Canadian charity that specializes in secure donation processing for charities across the country.

Can I set up a recurring gift? +

Yes — when you give through CanadaHelps, you can choose a one-time gift or set up automatic recurring giving on a schedule that works for you.

What is People's Church's CRA registration number? +

People's Church's Canada Revenue Number is 889304465RR0001.

Who can I talk to about giving? +

We'd love to help. Call the church office at 780-539-0572 or email mail@peopleschurchgp.com.
