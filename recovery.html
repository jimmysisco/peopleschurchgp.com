Celebrate Recovery

A safe, confidential place to find freedom from hurts, habits, and hang-ups — through a Christ-centered recovery program built on honesty, community, and grace.

Mondays · 6:30 PM

All Are Welcome

Whatever you're carrying — addiction, grief, anxiety, broken relationships, or habits you can't shake on your own — Celebrate Recovery is a place to bring it into the light. No one is asked to have it together. Every Monday evening, our doors are open to anyone ready to take an honest step toward healing.

What To Expect

A Simple, Welcoming Rhythm

Every gathering follows the same Christ-centered format, so there's never any guesswork about what a Monday night looks like.

Large Group

We start together with worship and a short teaching centered on one of the recovery principles.

Small Groups

We break into small, confidential groups to share honestly and support one another in a safe space.

Grace & Confidentiality

Whatever is shared in the room stays in the room. There's no cost, no judgment — just grace.
